# ConverSight.ai

> Agentic decision-intelligence platform for supply chain. ConverSight gives manufacturing, distribution, and retail organizations ready-to-deploy AI agents that automate planning, procurement, and optimization decisions, powered by the patented Athena AI engine over a Data Workbench, UniVerse knowledge layer, and workflow engine. Techstars-backed; serves 1,500+ supply chain organizations under SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance.
